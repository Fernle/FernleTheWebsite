Rating System

How I evaluate the games I play.

1. Core Loop & Game Feel

This is the heart of the experience. Beyond how a game looks, I focus on how its core action feels in my hands—whether that’s tight combat, clever puzzle-solving, or an addictive roguelike loop.

2. World, Pacing & Cohesion

Not every game tells a story, but every game builds a world and sets a rhythm. In narrative games, I look for deep writing; in systemic or arcade games, I evaluate progression pacing, atmosphere, and thematic cohesion.

3. Art Direction & Audio

This isn't about raw graphical power; it’s about identity. I check if the art style fits the game's soul, if the soundtrack elevates the mood, and if sound effects deliver satisfying feedback.

4. Polish, Clarity & UX

How frictionless is the experience? Instead of judging older or indie games by AAA tech standards, I look for clean UI, intuitive controls, stability, and how well the game respects my time.

5. The 'Spark' & Lasting Value

The signature factor. Does the game stay with me after I close it? I evaluate if it brings something fresh to its genre and whether the time invested was truly memorable.
